Look closer. Notice better.

Everyday sustainability often goes unnoticed. Nunnokku is an invitation to pause, observe, and recognise the small choices around us that make a difference.

Share what you noticed

What is Nunnokku?

Nunnokku encourages students to embrace sustainability by noticing meaningful practices around them and expressing their observations.

Observe

Look around and identify a meaningful sustainability practice.

Capture

Take an original photograph of what you observed.

Inspire

Explain why the observation matters and how it can inspire others.

What can you observe?

  • Plastic reduction
  • Water conservation
  • Energy efficiency
  • Waste reduction
  • Reuse & recycling
  • Sustainable transportation
  • Eco-friendly alternatives
  • Responsible consumption

Think beyond these examples. If you notice something meaningful, tell us about it.

How to participate

  1. 01

    Notice

    Find a sustainability-related practice around you.

  2. 02

    Capture

    Take a clear original photograph.

  3. 03

    Write

    Prepare a short reflection of approximately 100–150 words.

  4. 04

    Submit

    Submit your photograph and write-up together as a single PDF or Word document through the submission form below.

  5. 05

    Get featured

    Selected observations will be featured on the chapter's LinkedIn page.

Your 100–150 word reflection

In your write-up, cover:

  • What did you notice?
  • Where did you notice it?
  • Why is it sustainable?
  • Why did it catch your attention?
  • What can others learn from it?
